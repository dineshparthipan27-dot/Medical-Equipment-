Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our medical equipments, shipping, and warranty.

Do you provide installation for large equipments?

Yes! We have a team of expert bio-medical technicians who will visit your hospital/clinic to install large machineries like MRI scanners, ICU beds, and ventilators. We also provide a free demo.

What is the warranty period for your products?

All our medical equipments come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty. We also offer extended warranty packages up to 3 years covering both parts and labor.

Are your products ISO & CE certified?

Absolutely. Patient safety is our top priority. Every single product we sell is 100% genuine, clinically tested, and comes with ISO, CE, and FDA certifications where applicable.

Do you deliver to remote hospital locations?

Yes, we have a specialized medical logistics network that ensures safe and fast delivery of critical equipment to both urban hospitals and remote clinics across the country.
