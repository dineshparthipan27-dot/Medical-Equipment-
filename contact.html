Common Questions

Find quick answers about our equipment, services, and support.

Do you provide international shipping for medical equipment?

Yes, we ship FDA & CE approved equipment globally. All international shipments are fully insured and include customs clearance support.

How fast is your emergency repair service?

For AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) partners, we guarantee an on-site technician within 4 hours. Standard requests are addressed within 24 hours.

Do you offer training for new hospital setups?

Absolutely. Our biomedical engineers provide comprehensive on-site training for your staff to ensure safe and efficient operation of all installations.

Can I request a demo before purchasing?

Yes, we offer both virtual and in-person demonstrations for high-value machinery. Please contact our sales team to schedule a session with our experts.
